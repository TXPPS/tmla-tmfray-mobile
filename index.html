SELECTED TAPE TEMPO: 120 BPM

SYNC ON: GROVE SPACE/TIME, Tape Delay TIME, and FRAY CYCLE RATE use musical note divisions at the selected BPM and tape speed. Brake divisions follow BPM too. SYNC OFF: effects use free timing; brake uses a fixed 120 BPM reference.

This is the internal BPM reference, not DAW sync. A VST host transport connection is required for true DAW tempo sync. BPM does not retime your imported recording.

BPM sets the internal tempo reference (40–240). With SYNC enabled, ½ and 2× tape speed also halve or double the internal musical clock. This affects GROVE SPACE/TIME, FRAY CYCLE RATE, Tape Delay TIME, and the brake. PITCH ONLY preserves original tempo. SYNC is not DAW clock synchronization in this standalone HTML.

TAP sets BPM from your last few taps. Tap at least twice; the tempo display updates immediately.

DICE explores restrained FRAY / GROVE / TAPE sound variations. It leaves INPUT, OUTPUT, MASTER MIX, power, and speed unchanged. UNDO RANDOM is available in Settings.

Machine power has its own motor wind-down. Tape BRAKE latches at a stop until pressed again. Presets are managed in the notebook.
